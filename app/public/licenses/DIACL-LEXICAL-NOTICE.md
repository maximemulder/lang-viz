Reviewed exports: lexical database 2026-10-24T05:04:49Z; language tree
2026-10-23T06:58:27Z. `sources.json` supplies immutable recovery URLs, upstream
export URLs, exact sizes and SHA-256 hashes. The release also archives both raw
inputs independently of Git history.

Adaptations: the version 1 adapter selects lexeme-bearing language metadata,
plain text forms and notes, recorded relationships, citations, concepts and
collections; joins language-tree paths; normalizes omitted optional fields;
and reports incomplete focal points. It adds schema and source provenance.
It does not infer cognacy, migrations, or etymological relationships. Original
citations describe evidence in the database and do not imply endorsement.
